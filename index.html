Free Online Image Resizer & Compressor

Our free online image resizer and compressor lets you resize and compress images quickly without installing software. You can process JPG, PNG, and WebP images directly in your browser.

The image processing happens locally on your device using JavaScript and the HTML Canvas API. Your selected image does not need to be uploaded to a server.

How to Resize and Compress an Image

  1. Click Choose Image or drag an image into the upload area.
  2. Check the original image dimensions and file size.
  3. Enter your desired width and height.
  4. Keep Maintain Aspect Ratio enabled to preserve the original proportions.
  5. Adjust the compression quality using the quality slider.
  6. Select JPG, PNG, or WebP as the output format.
  7. Click Resize & Compress.
  8. Click Download Compressed Image to save the result.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this image compressor free?

Yes. This tool is designed to resize and compress images directly in your browser without requiring a paid subscription.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Image processing is performed locally in your browser using JavaScript and the Canvas API. The tool does not require an image upload server.

Which image formats are supported?

You can upload PNG, JPG, JPEG, and WebP images. You can also choose JPG, PNG, or WebP as the output format.
