Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Introduction

This privacy policy explains how QuickResizeTool (the "Website") handles information when you use our browser-based image resizer and compressor. The Website performs image processing locally in your web browser using standard web technologies (JavaScript & Canvas). We do not claim any company registration, address, or phone number.

Information We Collect

We strive to minimize any data collection. When you use the Website:

  • Images you select are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded to our servers by default.
  • We may collect non-personal technical data such as browser type, approximate device information, and anonymous usage metrics if analytics are enabled. This data does not contain image content.

How the Image Tool Works

The image processing (resizing, compression, format conversion) occurs entirely in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API and JavaScript. The selected image data stays on your device and within your browser during processing. Unless you explicitly use a feature that uploads images (none by default), images are never sent to any server.

Cookies

The Website uses minimal cookies or browser storage. Cookies (if used) are for basic functionality such as remembering theme preference. You can clear cookies using your browser settings.

Advertising and Third-Party Advertising

The Website may display third-party advertising (for example, Google AdSense). These third parties may use cookies and collect certain non-personal information to serve and personalise ads. We do not control the data collection of these third parties; please consult their privacy policies for details.

Google AdSense / Third-Party Ads

If the Website shows Google AdSense or similar ad networks, those services may collect advertising identifiers and use cookies to provide interest-based advertising. QuickResizeTool does not share image content with advertisers. Consult the respective ad provider's privacy policy for specifics.

Analytics

If analytics are enabled, we only collect aggregated, non-identifying usage statistics (e.g., number of visits, pages viewed, feature usage). No image content or personal data is included in these analytics by default.

Third-Party Links

The Website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites.

Data Security

We follow reasonable security practices for any data we store. Remember that browser-based tools offer privacy advantages because image processing happens locally; however, no method is 100% secure. If you are processing extremely sensitive images, take appropriate precautions.

Children's Privacy

The Website is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will reflect changes. Continued use of the Website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, use the Contact page on the Website. Contact details shown there are placeholders and can be updated later.
